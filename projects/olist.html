Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Analytics

An end-to-end e-commerce data analytics project analyzing Brazilian marketplace data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, delivery performance, product categories, reviews and business trends.

Python SQL Power BI

1. Executive Summary

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand overall marketplace performance and identify factors affecting sales, customer experience and operational performance.

The analysis combines Python for data cleaning and exploratory analysis, SQL for business-focused analysis and Power BI for interactive visualization and dashboard development.

The analysis covers approximately 99,441 orders and examines sales, customers, products, delivery performance, reviews, payments and order trends.

Total merchandise sales analyzed were approximately ₹13.59M, with strong contributions from categories such as Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts and Bed Bath & Table.

2. Project Objectives

  • Analyze overall sales and order performance.
  • Identify the top-performing product categories.
  • Understand customer and repeat-order behavior.
  • Compare sales performance across Brazilian states.
  • Analyze the relationship between delivery time and customer review scores.
  • Identify sales and order trends over time.
  • Analyze payment and customer behavior patterns.
  • Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

3. Data & Methodology

Data Used

The project uses multiple datasets from the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce marketplace covering orders, customers, products, order items, payments, reviews and product categories.

Dataset Records Purpose
Orders 99,441 Order status, purchase and delivery analysis
Order Items 112,652 Product sales and item-level analysis
Customers 99,443 Customer and geographic analysis
Products 31,953 Product and category analysis
Category Translation 72 English product category names

Data Preparation

Python was used to clean and prepare the datasets for analysis. Date columns were converted into datetime format, datasets were merged using relevant keys and additional analytical variables were created.

Delivery time was calculated using the difference between the order purchase timestamp and the customer delivery timestamp.

The delivery analysis showed a median delivery time of 10.22 days and an average delivery time of approximately 12.56 days.

Analytical Approach

Python was used for data cleaning, transformation, exploratory analysis and statistical investigation. SQL was used to answer business questions using joins, aggregation, filtering and conditional analysis. Power BI was then used to communicate the final insights through an interactive business dashboard.

Workflow: Raw Data → Python → SQL → Power BI → Insights → Recommendations

4. Key Findings & Visualizations

Finding 1 — Product Categories Drive Different Levels of Sales

Category-level analysis shows significant differences in sales contribution across the marketplace.

Among the strongest-performing categories were Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts and Bed Bath & Table.

Health & Beauty generated approximately ₹1.26M in merchandise sales, followed by Watches & Gifts at approximately ₹1.21M and Bed Bath & Table at approximately ₹1.04M.

Figure 1. Olist Product Category Sales Analysis

Finding 2 — Sales Performance Varies Across Brazilian States

Geographic analysis shows that sales are concentrated in certain Brazilian states.

São Paulo recorded approximately ₹5.20M in sales, making it a major contributor to overall marketplace revenue.

Comparing states using both total sales and average sales per order provides a more complete view of geographic performance.

Figure 2. Sales Performance Across Brazilian States

Finding 3 — Longer Delivery Times Are Associated With Lower Reviews

Delivery performance has a clear relationship with customer satisfaction.

Average review scores decline as delivery time increases. The average review score falls from approximately 4.45 for orders delivered within 0–5 days to approximately 3.35 for orders taking 25–29.145 days.

This indicates that longer delivery times represent an important area for operational improvement and customer experience management.

Figure 3. Relationship Between Delivery Time and Review Scores

Finding 4 — Repeat Customer Activity Increased Over Time

The analysis of customer ordering behavior shows growth in repeat-order activity over the observed period.

Repeat orders increased from approximately 36 in January 2017 to 277 in February 2018.

This trend provides an opportunity to examine customer retention, repeat purchasing patterns and cohort behavior more closely.

Figure 4. Repeat Customer and Order Trend Analysis

Finding 5 — Sales and Order Volume Do Not Always Move Together

Monthly analysis shows that sales value and order volume do not always change at the same rate.

This highlights the importance of monitoring multiple business metrics together rather than evaluating performance using sales or order count alone.

Average order value and item-level performance can provide additional context when investigating changes in overall revenue.

Figure 5. Sales, Orders and Business Performance Trends

6. Actionable Recommendations

1. Monitor Category Performance

Track category performance using total sales, item volume and average item value together to identify categories that are contributing the most to overall marketplace performance.

2. Investigate Long Delivery Times

Orders with extended delivery times should be monitored closely because longer delivery periods are associated with lower customer review scores.

3. Evaluate Geographic Performance

Analyze states using both total sales and average sales per order to distinguish between high-volume markets and markets with higher order value.

4. Monitor Sales, Orders and Average Order Value Together

Monthly business performance should be evaluated using sales, order volume and average order value together to better understand changes in marketplace performance.

5. Strengthen Customer Retention Analysis

Growing repeat-order activity provides an opportunity to use cohort and retention analysis to understand customer purchasing behavior and identify opportunities to increase repeat purchases.

6. Monitor Customer Experience

Delivery performance and review scores should be monitored together so that operational issues affecting customer satisfaction can be identified earlier.

7. Conclusion

The Olist analysis provides a detailed view of Brazilian e-commerce marketplace performance across sales, products, customers, geography, delivery and reviews.

The analysis identified strong category and geographic differences, with Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts and Bed Bath & Table among the major sales contributors and São Paulo representing a major geographic market.

Delivery performance also emerged as an important customer experience factor, with average review scores decreasing as delivery time increased.

The growth in repeat-order activity highlights the importance of customer retention and cohort analysis, while differences between sales and order trends show why multiple KPIs should be monitored together.

Overall, the project converts raw e-commerce data into practical business insights that can support decisions around product performance, geographic markets, delivery operations, customer experience and retention.
