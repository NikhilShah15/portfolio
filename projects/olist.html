Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Analytics

End-to-end customer, sales and operational performance analysis using the public Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset.

Python / PandasMySQLPower BIDAX

1. Executive Summary

I built this project to understand product demand, geographic sales performance, customer ordering behaviour, delivery performance, customer reviews, sales trends and payment behaviour.

The analysis generated six business questions and followed the workflow Business Problem → Python Analysis → SQL Validation → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights.

2. Business Questions

  • Which product categories are performing best?
  • Which states show the strongest sales performance?
  • Does delivery time relate to customer review scores?
  • How are sales changing over time?
  • How are new and repeated orders changing?
  • What factors are associated with changes in sales and order volume?

3. Key Findings

Category Performance

Health & Beauty generated approximately ₹1.26M, while Watches & Gifts generated approximately ₹1.21M. Their sales came from different combinations of item volume and item value.

State Performance

São Paulo generated approximately ₹5.20M, while smaller states could show higher average sales per order. Geographic performance therefore needs both scale and order-value context.

Delivery & Reviews

Average review scores declined from 4.45 for 0–5 day deliveries to 3.35 for 25–29.145 day deliveries. This is an association, not proof of causation.

Sales Over Time

Monthly merchandise sales reached approximately ₹1.01M in November 2017, the highest recorded month in the analysis.

New vs Repeated Orders

Repeated-order volume increased substantially over the observed period, but repeat-order volume alone does not establish an improved retention rate.

Sales vs Order Volume

Sales and order volume usually moved together, but selected periods moved in opposite directions, showing the importance of order value and category composition.

5. Business Recommendations

  • Monitor category performance using sales, item volume and average item value together.
  • Evaluate states using both total sales and average sales per order.
  • Investigate long-delivery orders by state, seller and category, and track delivery time with review scores.
  • Separate customer acquisition metrics from repeat-customer metrics and develop cohort-based retention measurement.
  • Monitor sales, orders and average order value together when interpreting month-to-month changes.
