Netflix Customer, Subscription & Engagement Analytics

End-to-end analysis using simulated customer data to understand customer account status, subscription plans, content preferences and viewing engagement.

PythonSQLPower BIExcel

Project Overview

The project follows the workflow Raw Data → Python → SQL → Power BI → Insights → Recommendations. The dataset is simulated/dummy data created for analytical and portfolio purposes.

Key Findings

Finding 1 — Customer Account Status

The analysis identified 697 active customers, 89 paused customers and 197 cancelled customers. The active base provides a strong foundation for engagement, while paused and cancelled customers represent opportunities for reactivation and churn reduction.

Finding 2 — Subscription Plans

The Premium plan has 341 customers, followed by Standard with 326 and Basic with 316. The relatively balanced distribution shows that no single tier dominates the customer base.

Finding 3 — Average Watch Hours by Genre

Horror has the highest average watch hours at 45 hours, followed by Sci-Fi at 44.91 hours. Overall genre averages range from 41.58 to 45 hours.

Finding 4 — Average Watch Hours Over Time

Average watch hours declined from 44.47 hours in 2022 to 41.70 hours in 2025. The rate of decline became smaller each year.

Finding 5 — TV Shows vs Movies

TV Shows have 507 users (51.6%), compared with Movies at 476 users (48.4%), showing a relatively balanced preference between the two content types.

Actionable Recommendations

  • Customer Retention & Reactivation: Focus on the 697 active customers while creating reactivation opportunities for 89 paused customers and learning from 197 cancellations.
  • Premium Subscription Growth: Build on the 341 Premium customers and encourage suitable lower-tier customers to adopt higher-value plans.
  • High-Engagement Content Focus: Give greater importance to highly engaged genres such as Horror and Sci-Fi.
  • Improve Viewing Engagement: Address the decline in average watch hours to stabilize customer engagement.
  • Balanced Content Strategy: Maintain a strong mix of Movies and TV Shows given their relatively balanced usage.
