## Featured Projects

### 🚲 Bike Rental Demand & Usage Analysis

An end-to-end data analysis project focused on understanding bike rental demand and customer behavior.

**Tools Used:**
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- SQL
- Tableau

**Analysis Covered:**
- Hourly rental demand
- Peak demand periods
- Seasonal demand patterns
- Temperature and demand relationship
- Weather impact on rentals
- Casual vs Registered customer behavior
- Weekday and hourly demand patterns

**Key Business Insights:**
- Demand has clear morning and evening peak periods.
- Fall records the highest average rental demand.
- Temperature is positively associated with rental demand.
- Weather conditions influence demand, particularly among casual users.
- Registered users form the majority of total rentals.
